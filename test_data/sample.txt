RAG_AI Sample Text Document

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is a technique that combines information retrieval with language model generation. Instead of relying solely on the model's training data, RAG fetches relevant documents from a knowledge base and uses them as context to generate more accurate and grounded responses.

Key benefits of RAG include:
- Reduced hallucination by grounding answers in retrieved documents.
- The ability to update knowledge without retraining the model.
- Transparency through source citations.
- Support for domain-specific question answering.

This plain text file is used to test the TXT ingestion loader in the RAG_AI pipeline.
